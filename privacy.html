What is stored

  • Your sign-in: your Google account's name, email address and picture, used only to sign you in and keep your athletes in your account.
  • Your athletes: what you type in: each athlete's name and club or school, the levels they compete, their routines and vaults, and for Masters their age decade. Many athletes are minors; enter only what you need to plan routines.
  • Without signing in nothing is saved. In local mode (?local), athletes are saved only in this browser.
