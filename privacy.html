Privacy

United Club Gymnastics' privacy policy applies to the Routine Planner. In short, for the planner:

  • Signing in uses your Google account's name, email address and picture.
  • Athletes you enter (names, clubs, levels, routines) are stored in Google Firebase. Only you, signed in, can read or change them.
  • On your device: a copy is kept so the planner works offline. Signing out removes it.
  • Your data: Export my data and Delete all my data are in the account menu (top right).
  • Feedback goes to UCG's feedback form and doesn't include athletes' names.

Questions: contact UCG.
